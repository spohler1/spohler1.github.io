Professional goals

From IT solutions to digital forensics

It's hard to capture in a paragraph what Seth intends to do after undergrad (partly because he's still not fully sure), but he has lightly carved out his professional intentions.

Career path

  1. 01 · First, after graduation

    IT solutions

    A few years in IT solutions work, similar to his experience on Florida's Medical Marijuana Use Registry.

    Already underway: as a Business Analyst Intern at Five Points Technology Group, Seth tests two State of Florida systems under HIPAA.

  2. 02 · The pivot

    Cybersecurity and digital forensics

    A move into cybersecurity, specifically digital forensics, potentially with a law enforcement agency such as the FBI or FDLE.

    Building toward it with a minor in Secure Computing and Networks, CompTIA Security+ (in progress) and packet analysis in CNT 3004.

  3. 03 · Later

    Cyber litigation or legislation

    A transition into cyber litigation or legislation, bringing justice and order to the technological landscape.

  4. 04 · Eventually

    Professor

    Ending his career as a professor, passing his knowledge and experience to the next generation of technicians and technical communicators.

The cybersecurity career he's aiming for

Within cybersecurity, Seth's target is digital forensics: recovering and analyzing digital evidence, ideally in a law enforcement setting such as the FBI or the Florida Department of Law Enforcement (FDLE).

Forensics also connects the rest of his plan. Evidence work leads naturally into cyber litigation and legislation, and years in the field are what he would eventually bring to a classroom.
