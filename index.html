Meet Seth

Seth is a junior at the University of Central Florida pursuing a B.S. in Information Technology. He started his technical journey as a Minecraft modder on the family computer at age 8, and his passion for computers and the digital world blossomed from there. As a teenager, Seth explored a variety of technical interests, from a children's stories podcast that taught him about RSS feeds and audio engineering to becoming the youngest member of the team responsible for creating and managing Florida's Medical Marijuana Use Registry (MMUR) in the spring semester of his senior year of high school.

Alongside his passion for technology is a deep love for communication and storytelling. His theatrical background gives him a strong skill set for all things presentation, and if you're lucky, he'll even sing you a little show tune! That led him to become vice president of Discomfort Club @ UCF (opens in a new tab), a registered student organization dedicated to ending the loneliness epidemic on campus through events designed to bring members out of their comfort zones and into community with peers they may not have met before. His role on the club's board has let him stretch his presentation and communication muscles by hosting events and keeping members informed, and it has given him the chance to put his technical skills to work by building custom tools and project management workflows for the board (more on the Certifications & Activities page).

After a few years in IT solutions work, Seth hopes to pivot into cybersecurity, specifically digital forensics, and later into cyber litigation or legislation. Read the full plan on the Professional Goals page.
